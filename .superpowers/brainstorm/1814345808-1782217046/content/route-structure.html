Three Map Canvas Pages

Campus Map

/studio/campuses/[id]/map

Boundary, buildings, outdoor paths, gates. MapLibre with OSM/satellite.

Building Config

/studio/campuses/[id]/buildings/[id]

Floor list, metadata (name, color, photo), 2.5D preview. No map canvas.

Floor Editor

/studio/campuses/[id]/buildings/[id]/floors/[id]

Asset library, floor plan canvas, properties. Rooms, hallways, stairs, QR, panoramas.
